Safety systems · Austria

Austrian main signals say whether and how fast you may pass (Halt, Frei, Frei mit 40, Frei mit 60); distant signals (Vorsignale) what the next main signal shows. Other speeds come from speed indicators beside the signal. On the Semmeringbahn (1116, 4024) PZB 90 watches the signals; on the Mittenwaldbahn (1020, 1989) it is the older Indusi I60, and the German section to the border has German signals (see the Germany manual). Speeds in km/h. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's ÖBB profiles: the same layout as the German trains.

Safety systems

PZB 90
Punktförmige Zugbeeinflussung

Magnets at 1000 Hz (distant signal), 500 Hz (before some main signals; rare in Austria) and 2000 Hz (main signal). Acknowledge, then keep within the speed curve.

WhereSemmeringbahn: 1116, 4024
QuadrantENG 2 Wachsam, ENG 1 Frei, round aux Befehl 40; right aux square PZB on / off
Indusi I60
Induktive Zugsicherung (1989)

After Wachsam (4 s) be at or below 95 (O) / 75 (M) km/h within 34 s: one check, no curve. 500 Hz: a spot check at 40. Frei only releases a penalty brake.

WhereMittenwaldbahn: 1020
QuadrantSame buttons: ENG 2 Wachsam, ENG 1 Frei, round aux Befehl; right aux Indusi
SiFa
Sicherheitsfahrschaltung

1116 / 4024: after 30 s a lamp, 2.5 s later a buzzer, 2.5 s later the emergency brake. 1020: no lamp: a buzzer after 30 s, an alarm 75 m later, the brake 75 m after that.

LZB / ETCS
Continuous systems

LZB on parts of the Westbahn and ETCS on new lines in the real world; neither on these TSW routes.

How to drive

Approaching Vorsicht

  1. PZB / Indusi sounds at the distant signal: press Wachsam (ENG 2) within 4 s.
  2. Brake: PZB 90 by its curve; Indusi to 95 / 75 within 34 s.
  3. Stop before the main signal at Halt.

Passing Halt with an order

  1. Ersatzsignal or a written order only.
  2. Hold Befehl (round aux) while the front passes the 2000 Hz magnet, at max 40.
  3. Max 40 through the points.

SiFa

  1. Reset with the gear lever when it warns (1116 / 4024 lamp; 1020 buzzer).
  2. Missed: emergency brake; stop, reset, release.
Before departure: All three load with SiFa and PZB / Indusi off: switch both on (aux squares lit). Set the train type: in the cab on the 1116 / 4024; on the 1020 the Indusi mode knob is in the compartment near cab 2.
