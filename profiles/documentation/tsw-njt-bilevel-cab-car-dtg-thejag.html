Multi-Level cab car

Cab setup

  • Reverser handle in (PARK BRK up; the car loads with it in); pantograph up on the loco (ENG 2)
  • ACSES / ASES and alerter in (aux squares lit; both load cut out)
  • Lights to Bright
  • Handle at Idle, automatic brake at Lap or Service (parked); reverser from Neutral to Forward

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Handle away from you through Idle into P1..P max
  • Acknowledge the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle into B1..B max (the loco's dynamic / blended brake), automatic brake to Service and Lap; Emergency at the far end
