Class 483

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser key and master key in; Start (circuit breaker) in the cab
  • Reverser from Off to Forward 1
  • Lights to Head on
  • Brake handle at EP max (parked)
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, wait for the guard signal, check the signal
  • Brake handle through the EP zone to Released
  • Traction controller to Shunting, then Full series and Full parallel
  • Keep the deadman (traction handle) pressed; acknowledge with the gear lever if the game asks
  • Brake: traction Off, handle into the EP zone towards EP max; Westinghouse hold and charge beyond it, Emergency at the far end
