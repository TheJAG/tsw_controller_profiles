QA pendingLondon Underground · Dovetail Games · TCA Quadrant Airbus

1972 Tube Stock

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: selector key and control switch key in, control switch on, deadman held (the controller is dead without them)
  • Direction selector from Off to Forward
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Controller at Service (parked); Shutdown stays in the cab

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Controller to Off / release, then Shunt, Series, Parallel
  • Brake: back through Hold into the EP notches; Lap holds, Service and Emergency at the far end
  • Reset the valve with the gear lever after a deadman or tripcock trip; tripcock reset on ENG 1
