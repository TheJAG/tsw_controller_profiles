Gallery cab car

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, automatic brake cut in, engine run / generator field / control and fuel pump on (the car loads with them off)
  • Alerter valve cut in (left aux lit), EP brake on (right aux lit)
  • Lights to Bright, crossing lights on
  • Independent applied (round aux lit), automatic brake at Service (parked); throttle Idle, dynamic Off; reverser to Forward

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; round aux out to release the independent, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch; keep the dynamic brake Off while powering
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, dynamic brake past Setup into the zone; automatic brake to Service, Lap to hold, Emergency at the far end
