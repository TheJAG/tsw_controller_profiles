DBpbzfa 766.2

Cab setup

  • Battery on in the cab (the car loads cold, keys out)
  • Reverser key and brake key in (PARK BRK up)
  • Richtungsschalter from Off to Neutral
  • Pantograph and Hauptschalter of the loco from the cab (PantographControl, CircuitBreaker)
  • SiFa and PZB fuses on (aux squares), PZB mode set
  • Lights to Headlights; open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, check the signal and PZB
  • Richtungsschalter to Forward
  • Führerbremsventil to Running, wait for the brakes to release
  • Fahrschalter towards Z
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Führerbremsventil towards Full service in steps; the dynamic brake stays in the cab
