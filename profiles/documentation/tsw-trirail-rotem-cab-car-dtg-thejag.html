Rotem cab car

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, cab set-up key on, automatic brake cut in, generator field on (the car loads with them off)
  • ATS and ATC cut in (left aux lit); lights to Bright; hand brake off (round aux)
  • Handle at Off, automatic brake at Full service (parked); reverser to Forward (it only moves with the handle at Off)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release (the lever ramps through its gates)
  • Handle away from you: 1 … 8
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever, acknowledge ATS with ENG 2
  • Brake: handle back through Off and Setup into the dynamic zone (not in Neutral); automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end
