Safety systems · Japan

Japanese signals give a speed class for the section ahead. On the TSW Tadami Line (single track, 65 km/h) the main signals have three aspects, Clear, Caution (45 km/h) and Stop, with a four-lamp distant signal before each station and repeaters where the view is short. The JR East KiHa 40 runs under ATS-SN and the EB vigilance device. Busier JR lines add Reduce speed and Restricted speed on five-lamp heads (shown for reference). Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's KiHa 40 profile; "gear lever" means the Quadrant's gear lever (the KiHa's own gear is on the flap lever).

Safety systems

ATS-SN
自動列車停止装置 S type, N variant

A coil about 600 m before a signal at stop rings the bell: brake and press ATS Acknowledge (確認扱い) within 5 s, or the emergency brake applies. The alert sound then continues. A coil at the signal brakes at once if it is passed at stop, and a departure coil does the same if you start against a starting signal at stop.

WhereThe whole Tadami Line in TSW
QuadrantGear lever = ATS Acknowledge; left aux = ATS and EB power; round aux = Alert Release (警報持続解除)
ATS-P / Ps
Pattern-type ATS

Checks a braking curve to signals and restrictions and brakes automatically; no acknowledge. On the real line Ps covers the Aizu-Wakamatsu and Koide station areas; TSW simulates SN only.

WhereP: Tokyo area and main lines. Ps: JR East regional lines (Sendai, Niigata areas)
EB
EB装置 (Emergency Brake device)

Above 15 km/h, with no throttle, brake or horn movement for about 60 s, a buzzer and lamp: reset within about 5 s or the emergency brake applies.

WhereMost JR trains, the KiHa 40 included
QuadrantLeft throttle button = EB Reset

How to drive under ATS-SN and the EB

ATS-SN: approaching a stop signal

  1. About 600 m before a signal at stop the bell rings.
  2. Brake handle to Lap or Apply, and within 5 s press ATS Acknowledge (gear lever).
  3. The alert sound continues: stop before the signal.
  4. Once stopped (or when the signal clears) press Alert Release (round aux), then drive on.

ATS emergency brake

  1. Passing a signal at stop, or no acknowledge in time: emergency brake.
  2. Brake handle to Emergency, wait for a complete stop.
  3. Press ATS Acknowledge (gear lever).
  4. Release the brake once the brake pipe has recovered; wait for the signal.

EB (vigilance)

  1. Above 15 km/h, moving the throttle, brake or horn resets the timer.
  2. After about 60 s without one the EB buzzer sounds: press EB Reset (left throttle button).
  3. Missed: emergency brake. Brake handle to Emergency, the warning stops; then release.

Before departure

  1. ATS and EB power on (left aux): the self-test rings the bell for 2 s, then the alert sound.
  2. Press Alert Release (round aux) to silence it.
  3. Check the starting signal and the departure time, close the doors (RUD TRIM), release the brake, notch up.

Single track

  • The Tadami Line is single track with crossing loops (Nishi-Wakamatsu, Aizu-Bange, Aizu-Miyashita, Aizu-Kawaguchi).
  • At a loop the starting signal governs: wait for it to clear.
  • The departure coil brakes the train if you start against it.

Speed

  • Line speed 65 km/h; Caution 45 km/h.
  • Speed signs set curve and points limits; higher speed only once the rear has passed the end sign.
  • ATS-SN does not check your speed: brake to stop yourself.
Remember: ATS-SN only checks that you acknowledged; it does not watch your speed afterwards. The coil at the signal is the last line of defence and stops the train at once.
