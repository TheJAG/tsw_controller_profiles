Class 750

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, BS2 key unlocked (the loco loads with both out and the valve in Cutout)
  • Reverser to Engine only, ENG 1 up for about 8 s until the engine runs, then ENG 1 down
  • Train protection on (left aux lit); it loads in Banking
  • Lights to Head full
  • Independent at Full; brake valve from Cutout to Neutral

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; reverser to Forward
  • Brake valve to Release (Quick release at the far end), independent to Release
  • Controller away from you: 1 … 8 (it only moves with the reverser out of Off)
  • Acknowledge vigilance with the gear lever
  • Brake: controller back to 0, brake valve into the apply zone; Emergency nearest you
