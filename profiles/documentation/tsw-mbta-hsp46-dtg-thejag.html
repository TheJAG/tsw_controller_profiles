HSP46

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: generator field on (loads off). Flick ENG 2 up and down (reverser handle in, then out) to unlock the reverser; it locks again every time it passes Neutral
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; ACSES / ATC and alerter on (aux squares lit); HEP in the cab
  • Lights to Bright with ditch lights
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); throttle Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; independent to Released, round aux to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, automatic brake into the service zone, independent for the final stop; Emergency at the far end
