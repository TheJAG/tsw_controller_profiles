F7

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: engine run, generator field and fuel pump / control switches on (the loco loads with them off; no key)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; brake valve cock cut in (left aux lit)
  • Lights to Bright
  • Independent applied (round aux lit), 24RL brake at Service (parked); throttle Idle, transition Off

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • 24RL brake to Running; round aux out to release the independent
  • Transition lever to Series parallel (throttle at Idle), then throttle notch by notch; SP shunt at speed
  • Hold the safety pedal with the gear lever when it calls
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, transition to Braking and throttle up for dynamic braking; 24RL to First service or Service, Lap to hold; Emergency at the far end
