Flying Scotsman

Cab setup

  • Brake pin in (PARK BRK up); the AI fireman keeps the fire, dampers and firebox door
  • Cut-off to full forward gear (far end), AWS on (left aux)
  • Blower open (ENG 1) while standing; live injector (ENG 2) when the water drops
  • Cylinder cocks open (round aux lit) for the first turns of the wheels
  • Steam brake at Full, vacuum brake at Full service (parked); regulator Closed

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Vacuum brake to Running, steam brake Off
  • Regulator open a little, then more; wind the cut-off back towards Mid-gear as speed rises
  • Cylinder cocks closed after the first 100 m; blower closed once the exhaust draws the fire
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever; brake with the vacuum valve in the service zone, Emergency at the far end
