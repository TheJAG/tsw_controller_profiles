BR 363

Cab setup

  • Master key on (PARK BRK); engine start, fuel pump and the Stufe selector (Long distance / Shunting) in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares)
  • Lights to Front 3 lamps, or Front + rear for shunting
  • Zusatzbremse at Hold, Führerbremsventil at Lap
  • The profile drives the left desk (Throttle_L, DriversBrakeValve_L): sit at that desk

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal and PZB, release the handbrake in the cab
  • Führerbremsventil to Driving (far end), wait for the brakes to release
  • Zusatzbremse to Release
  • Fahrschalter to Stufe 1, then Stufe 2 (one notch per step)
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Führerbremsventil to Service, or the Zusatzbremse towards Apply (spring, pulse it)
