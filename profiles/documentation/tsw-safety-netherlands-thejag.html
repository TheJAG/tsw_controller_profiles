Safety systems · Netherlands

Dutch signals show the speed, not the route: green, yellow or red, with a lit number × 10 km/h below. Flashing green = the speed applies from this signal; yellow + number = reach it by the next signal; a flashing number or flashing yellow are special cases (see the cards). In TSW 7: NS ICMm and NS SNG on Zwolle – Groningen, under ATB-EG, ATB-Vv and the deadman. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's profiles.

Safety systems

ATB-EG
Automatische Treinbeïnvloeding, eerste generatie

Coded track circuits set a ceiling of 40, 60, 80, 130 or 140 km/h. Overspeed rings a bell; brake within ~3 s or ATB brakes.

WhereWhole Zwolle – Groningen line; ICMm and SNG
QuadrantLeft aux square: ATB fuse (ICMm) / ATB / ERTMS cut-out (SNG)
ATB-Vv
ATB verbeterde versie

Beacons 120, 30 and 3 m before selected red signals impose a braking curve to a stop. Acts only under code [–] or [BD].

WhereSelected stop signals on Zwolle – Groningen
QuadrantShares the ATB fuse / cut-out (left aux square)
Deadman
Dodemansinrichting

Driver-alive check. Answer the warning in time or the train makes an emergency brake application.

WhereICMm and SNG
QuadrantGear lever up = acknowledge (game key Q); right aux square = deadman fuse / cut-out
ATB-NG · ETCS
ATB nieuwe generatie · European Train Control System

ATB-NG (balises, 10 km/h steps) on Dutch diesel lines; ETCS on Betuweroute, HSL-Zuid, Hanzelijn. Not in TSW 7.

WhereNot on any TSW Dutch route

Main signal aspects (hoofdseinen)

Lamps top to bottom: green, yellow, red; white number below. Dwarf signals: same colours, max 40 km/h.
Green (groen)
Proceed at the local (line) speed. ATB code [13] or [14].
DriveAccelerate to the speed on the boards.
Flashing green
Proceed at max 40 km/h from this signal. ATB code [–] 40.
DriveBe at 40 km/h passing the signal; usually a diverging route.
Flashing green + 8
Proceed at max 80 km/h from this signal (number × 10).
DriveHold 80 km/h until a speed board lets you go faster.
Yellow (geel)
Next signal may be red. Limit speed to 40 km/h. ATB code [–] 40.
DriveBrake now to 40 km/h and be ready to stop at the next signal.
Yellow + 8
Reduce to 80 km/h, reached before the next signal. ATB code [8].
DriveBrake to 80 km/h before the next signal.
Yellow + flashing 6
Limit to 60 km/h; short section, may not be reachable by the next signal.
DriveBrake to 60; keep braking if the next signal asks for less.
Flashing yellow
Drive on sight (rijden op zicht): track beyond may be occupied. Max 40 km/h.
DriveCreep, max 40 km/h, able to stop short of any obstruction.
Red (rood)
Stop before the signal. ATB-EG does not stop you; ATB-Vv may.
DriveStop short of the signal; wait for a proceed aspect.
Passing red: ATB-EG never enforces below 40 km/h, so it lets you pass a red signal at up to 40 km/h. Stop short yourself; in TSW only ATB-Vv beacons can save you, and only at selected signals.

Boards and other signals

Board numbers are × 10 km/h.
P-sein
Automatic block signal (P-bord). Red still means stop.
DriveCall the signaller; pass on sight only if no contact is possible.
Speed reduction
Snelheidsverminderingsbord: lower limit ahead.
DriveBe at 80 km/h by the next white speed board.
Speed board
Snelheidsbord: local speed beyond the board.
DriveLower: from the front. Higher: once the rear has passed.
Line speed
Green triangle (sein 316): line speed may be resumed.
DriveAccelerate once the rear of the train has passed.
Train length
Treinlengtebord: stop marker for trains of up to this many cars.
DriveStop at the board for your train length. Which board a unit uses depends on the train; see its manual.
Departure light
Vertrekseinlicht: the departure procedure may start.
DriveWait for it (and a proceed aspect) before the doors close.

How to drive under ATB-EG and the deadman

Code drops (speed reduction)

  1. A single chime and the cab display shows the lower code, after a short random delay in TSW.
  2. Already below the new ceiling: nothing to do.
  3. Above it (+5 km/h): a continuous bell. Brake within about 3 s.
  4. Make at least a minimum brake application (ICMm step 1 or more, SNG min brake or more); the bell stops.
  5. Three short bells below ceiling +5: you may release the brake.
Brake on time for the signals anyway: ATB only checks you brake, not that you stop in time.

ATB brake (forced brake)

  1. No brake within ~3 s, or overspeed: ATB brakes to a stop; red ATB lamp lit.
  2. When stopped, set full brake on the right throttle: ICMm step 7, SNG Max brake.
  3. Press Ontgrendelen (unlock) in the cab with the mouse; it is not on the Quadrant.
  4. Wait for the red lamp to go out, then release and drive on.

ATB-Vv at red signals

  • Acts only under code [–] or [BD]; the beacons are not shown to you.
  • After the 30 m beacon the limit is 10 km/h (+5).
  • Over the braking curve: forced brake to a stop.
  • A release loop up to 60 m before the signal, or a new code above [–], lifts the curve.

Deadman (dodemansinrichting)

  1. Keep the deadman fuse on: right aux square (ICMm fuse, SNG cut-out in).
  2. When the warning sounds, lift the gear lever (game key Q).
  3. Miss it and the train brakes; acknowledge, then release and drive on (TSW behaviour not yet verified).

Before departure

  1. Left aux square on: ATB fuse (ICMm) or ATB / ERTMS cut-out set to cut in (SNG). Off gives [BD] and five bells.
  2. Right aux square on: deadman fuse / cut-in.
  3. Check the cab code matches the signal, then pull away.

Boards to know

  • Verkenbord: platform ahead at braking distance; start braking for the stop.
  • CODE board: ATB-EG / ATB-NG changeover at the route fringes (ATB-NG itself is not simulated).
  • A / E boards: start and end of a temporary speed restriction.
