DB Regio · Train Sim Germany · TCA Quadrant Airbus

BR 218

Cab setup

  • Battery, engine groups 1 and 2, compressor and the gear (Fast / Slow) in the cab
  • PARK BRK up: reverser key and brake key in
  • SiFa and PZB fuses on (aux squares), PZB mode in the cab
  • Richtungsschalter from Off to Forward
  • Lights to Signal, normal (coach lighting with HI)
  • Zusatzbremse at Hold, Führerbremsventil at Full service

Cleared to depart

  • Doors: RUD TRIM left / right unlocks a side while held, release locks
  • Führerbremsventil to Running (far end), then Off once released
  • Zusatzbremse to Release
  • Fahrstufen: push the handwheel up a few steps at a time, 0 to 15
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Fahrstufen to 0, Führerbremsventil to 40..70 %; the hydrodynamic brake stays in the cab
