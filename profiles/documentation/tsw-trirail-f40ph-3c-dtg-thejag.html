Tri-Rail · Dovetail Games · TCA Quadrant Airbus

F40PH-3C

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: lead set-up (handle, field, brake valve, MU-2A, traction interlock; loads trailing)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward with the throttle at Idle
  • ATC / PTC and alerter fuse on (aux squares lit; both load off)
  • Lights to High
  • Automatic brake at Full service (parked), throttle Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; independent to Released, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, automatic brake into the service zone, independent for the final stop; Emergency at the far end
