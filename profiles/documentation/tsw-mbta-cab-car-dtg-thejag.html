CTC-3 cab car

Cab setup

  • Throttle at Idle, then PARK BRK up: reverser key in (Neutral), automatic brake cut in, generator field on
  • Alerter and cab signal fuses on (aux squares lit); lights to Bright; hand brake off (round aux)
  • Automatic brake at Full service (parked); reverser to Forward

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Throttle notch by notch (the F40PH / HSP46 at the back follows)
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever, acknowledge cab signals with ENG 2
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end. No independent or dynamic brake on the cab car
