F125

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, generator field on, brake mode to Passenger (the loco loads in Test with the field off); HEP in the cab
  • Handle at Idle, then reverser from Neutral to Forward (it only moves at Idle)
  • PTC / ATS fuses and alerter on (aux squares lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on (round aux)
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release (the lever ramps through the gates); independent to Released, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Handle away from you: N1 … N8
  • Reset the alerter and the ATS with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle and Setup into the dynamic zone; automatic brake into the service zone (Suppression sits mid-zone); Emergency at the far end
