SD70ACe

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, engine run / generator field / fuel pump on (the loco loads with them off)
  • Alerter and cab signal fuses on (aux squares lit); lights to Bright
  • Independent applied (round aux lit; it loads released), automatic brake at Full service (parked); throttle Idle, dynamic Off
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward, then nudge the throttle at Idle: it frees the dynamic brake (a lagging interlock)

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; round aux out to release the independent (no bail-off through the API)
  • Throttle notch by notch; the throttle is locked while the dynamic brake is out of Off
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever; ENG 2 acknowledges the cab signal
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, dynamic brake past Set up into the zone; automatic brake into the service zone for the stop, Emergency at the far end
