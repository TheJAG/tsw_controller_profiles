MP36PH-3C

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, brake valve cut in, engine run / generator field / control switches on (the loco loads with them off); MU-2A valve to Lead and HEP to Run in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; alerter breaker on (left aux lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); handle at Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release, independent to Release (ENG 2 up to bail off)
  • Handle away from you: P1 … P8
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle and Setup into the dynamic notches; automatic brake into the service zone for the stop, Emergency at the far end
