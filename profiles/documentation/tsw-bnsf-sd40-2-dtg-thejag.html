SD40-2

Cab setup

  • Reverser handle in (PARK BRK up); isolation switch at Run in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; dynamic brake cut in and alerter fuse closed (aux squares lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on
  • Independent applied (round aux lit), automatic brake at Full service (parked); throttle Idle, dynamic Off

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; round aux out to release the independent, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch; keep the dynamic brake Off while powering
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, dynamic brake past Set up into the zone; automatic brake into the service zone for the stop, Emergency at the far end
