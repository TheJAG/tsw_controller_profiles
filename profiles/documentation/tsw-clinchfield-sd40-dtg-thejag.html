SD40

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, engine run / generator field / control switches on, cut-off pilot valve at Freight (the loco loads with all of them off); MU-2A valve to Lead in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; alerter fuse closed and dynamic brake breaker closed (aux squares lit)
  • Selector: hold the mode switch right about half a second per step, OFF to 1 (the throttle is dead in OFF)
  • Lights to Bright front
  • Independent applied (round aux lit), 26L brake at Full service (parked); throttle Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • 26L brake to Release; round aux out to release the independent, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch; selector up to 2, 3, 4 as the speed rises
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Dynamic braking: throttle to Idle, selector down past OFF to B (reverser locked in B), flap lever for the strength; 26L into the service zone for the stop, Emergency at the far end
