1938 Tube Stock

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser key, master key and key lever in, deadman held
  • Reverser from Off to Forward 1 (Forward 2 for full speed)
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Driver’s brake at EP max (parked), controller Off

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Brake to Released; controller to Shunting, then Full series, Full parallel
  • Brake: EP hold to EP max for the stop; Westinghouse hold and charge beyond; Emergency at the far end
  • Cold start on ENG 1 if the train is dead
