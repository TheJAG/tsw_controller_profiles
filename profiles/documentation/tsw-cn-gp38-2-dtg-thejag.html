GP38-2

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, generator field / engine run / control switches on, brake valve cut in to Freight (the loco loads with them off)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; alerter fuse on (left aux lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on (round aux)
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); throttle Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; independent to Release, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, automatic brake into the service zone, independent for the final stop; Emergency at the far end
