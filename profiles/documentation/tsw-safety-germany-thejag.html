Safety systems · Germany

German signals mostly show what to expect: a main signal (Hauptsignal) says whether you may pass it and at what speed, a distant signal (Vorsignal) what the next main signal shows. Speeds on signals and boards are number × 10 km/h. Four signal families appear on the TSW 7 routes: H/V (West), Ks (new lines), Hl (former DR, e.g. Leipzig – Dresden) and semaphores. PZB 90 watches every signal, LZB takes over on high-speed lines, and the SiFa checks that you are awake. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's German profiles.

Safety systems

PZB 90
Punktförmige Zugbeeinflussung

Track magnets at 1000 Hz (distant signal), 500 Hz (about 250 m before the main signal, not at every signal) and 2000 Hz (main signal). Acknowledge, then brake within the speed curve or PZB brakes.

WhereEvery German route
QuadrantENG 2 Wachsam (acknowledge), ENG 1 Frei (release), round aux Befehl 40; right aux square PZB on / off
LZB
Linienzugbeeinflussung

Continuous cab signalling by cable loop: target speed and distance up to 13 km ahead on the cab display; lineside signals may be dark. Above 160 km/h only under LZB.

WhereHigh-speed lines, e.g. Kassel – Würzburg, Nürnberg – Ingolstadt, Köln – Aachen, Leipzig – Dresden
QuadrantLZB orders and Ende: ENG 1 (Frei); nothing to press when LZB takes over
SiFa
Sicherheitsfahrschaltung

Driver vigilance: a lamp after about 30 s without a SiFa action, a buzzer 2.5 s later, an emergency brake 2.5 s after that.

WhereEvery German train
QuadrantGear lever = SiFa reset; left aux square SiFa on / off
ETCS
European Train Control System

Replacing PZB / LZB on new lines in the real world.

How to drive under PZB 90, LZB and SiFa

1000 Hz: distant signal at caution

  1. Passing a Vr 0 / Vr 2 / Ks 2 / Ks 1 flashing with Zs 3v ≤ 6 / Hl 7 / Hl 10 the PZB sounds: press Wachsam (ENG 2) within 4 s.
  2. The 1000 Hz lamp lights for 1250 m: be below the train-type speed within the time.
  3. Brake to the signal's speed or to a stop.
  4. If the signal clears, press Frei (ENG 1) after 700 m to end the check.

500 Hz and 2000 Hz: approaching red

  1. 250 m before a red main signal the 500 Hz magnet checks a falling curve to 45 / 35 / 25 km/h.
  2. Stop before the signal: the 2000 Hz magnet brakes at once if you pass it at stop.
  3. Frei cannot end the 500 Hz check.

Befehl 40: passing a red signal

  1. Only with Zs 1, Zs 7 or a written order.
  2. Hold Befehl 40 (round aux) while the front passes the 2000 Hz magnet.
  3. Max 40 km/h to the next main signal; on sight only with Zs 7 or when ordered.

Emergency brake (Zwangsbremsung)

  1. The train stops; the flashing lamp shows why: yellow 1000 Hz, red 500 Hz, white Befehl 40.
  2. At a stand, press Frei (ENG 1).
  3. Release the brake and drive on, restrictive (45 / 25 km/h) where the check is still running.

Start and restrictive mode

  • After switching on or a stop inside a check, PZB runs restrictive: the 70 and 85 lamps flash alternately.
  • Start programme: max 45 km/h for 550 m; Frei ends it when no check is running.
  • Pick the train type in the cab (O passenger, M / U freight).

LZB

  • Entering: Ü and B light; nothing to press.
  • Follow V-Soll on the display; signals may be dark. Leave the AFB (flap lever, where fitted) at line speed: LZB lowers the set-point itself.
  • Ende flashes about 1700 m before the exit: press Frei (ENG 1) within 10 s. At the exit Ü goes out and PZB rules again.
  • Ü flashing (transmission lost) or V 40: Frei, then max 40 (V 40 on sight). E 40: no button, max 40.

SiFa

  1. Keep the SiFa on (left aux square).
  2. When the SiFa lamp lights, flick the gear lever; any SiFa action resets the timer.
  3. Missed: emergency brake; stop, reset the SiFa, release and drive on.
Before departure: SiFa and PZB on: aux squares lit on most locos, unlit where the square is the Störschalter or isolation switch (ICE, S-Bahn 42x, 612); see the train's manual. Set the right train type, check the signal. With PZB restrictive after start-up keep below 45 km/h until the check ends.
