ABe 8/12 Allegra

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK up); battery and pantograph in the cab
  • Reverser from 0 to Forward
  • Train protection and vigilance on (aux squares)
  • Lights to Full
  • Zugbremse at Full service, handle at Max brake (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Zugbremse to Release, handle to 0
  • Handle past Min power into the power zone; set a Vmax on the flap lever if you use the cruise control
  • Acknowledge the SISTE with the gear lever, the ZS with ENG 1
  • Brake: handle back through 0 into the brake zone; Zugbremse into the service zone; Emergency at the far ends
