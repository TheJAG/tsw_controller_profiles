M7

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: master key in, deadman held; the reverser loads at Key Out
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • ATC / ACSES and alerter cut in (aux squares lit); check their state in the cab, the levers load at 0
  • Lights to Bright
  • Controller at Max brake (parked); move it off Emergency before the reverser

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; buzzer (ENG 1)
  • Controller towards you through Min brake and Coast into the power zone
  • Acknowledge ATC / ACSES with the gear lever
  • Brake: controller away from you through Coast and Min brake into the brake zone; Max brake for the stop, Emergency at the far end
