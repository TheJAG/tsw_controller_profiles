Class 843

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: master key to Master, brakes cut in (the railcar loads with the key Off and the brakes cut out)
  • Engines: reverser to Engine only, ENG 1 up until they run (most services start with them running)
  • LS90 on (left aux lit); it loads isolated
  • Lights to Head full
  • Independent at Applied, handle at V

Cleared to depart

  • Close the doors (RUD TRIM again), check the signal; reverser to Forward
  • Independent to Released
  • Handle: tap into S and back to J to build power step by step; J holds it, V coasts
  • Acknowledge vigilance with the gear lever
  • Brake: tap into BE (dynamic) or BP (air) and back to V, which holds the brake; J releases it; R emergency nearest you
