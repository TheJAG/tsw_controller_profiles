Class 484

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK); the EP key and the circuit breaker (MLCB) in the cab
  • Reverser from Off to Neutral
  • Check the AWS and DSD isolation lights are out; DRA in the cab
  • Headlights to Day
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors and check the signal
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Handle from Full service through Off to P1
  • Notch up to P7 as speed rises
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever
  • Brake: back through Off to B1..Full service
