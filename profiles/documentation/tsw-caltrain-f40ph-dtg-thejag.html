F40PH-2CAT

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, brake valve cut in, engine run / generator field / control switches on (the loco loads with them off); isolation switch at Run and HEP in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; alerter on (left aux lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on
  • Independent applied (round aux lit), automatic brake at Service (parked); throttle Idle, dynamic Off

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; round aux out to release the independent, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Throttle notch by notch; keep the dynamic brake Off while powering
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, dynamic brake past Setup into the zone; automatic brake to Service, Lap to hold, Emergency at the far end
