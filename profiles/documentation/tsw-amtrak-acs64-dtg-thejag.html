ACS-64

Cab setup

  • Reverser handle in (PARK BRK up); pantograph up (ENG 2), MCB closed (round aux)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • ACSES / ATC and alerter on (aux squares lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); handle at Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release, independent to Release
  • Handle away from you into the throttle zone
  • Acknowledge the alerter and cab signals with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle into the dynamic zone, automatic brake into the service zone (Suppression sits mid-zone); Emergency at the far end
