NS SNG

Cab setup

  • Insert master key (PARK BRK)
  • Handle fully back at Max brake (right throttle)
  • ATB / ERTMS and deadman cut-outs in (aux squares)
  • Signal lights On (AUTO BRK), intensity on the flap lever
  • Release the parking brake (right throttle button)
  • Unlock the doors

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors
  • Check signal and ATB
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Handle forward through min brake to 0, then 5 % and continuous power
  • Brake: back past 0 into min brake, continuous to Max brake
