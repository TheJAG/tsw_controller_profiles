Acela

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, cab activated; pantograph up (ENG 2)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • ACSES / ATC and alerter cut in (aux squares lit); the train loads with them cut out
  • Lights to Full, ditch lights on
  • Automatic brake at Full service (parked), throttle Off, cruise Off

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Throttle to Ready, then into the effort zone; cruise control on the flap lever if wanted
  • Acknowledge the alerter and cab signals with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Ready, automatic brake into the service zone, Suppression for a penalty; Emergency at the far end
