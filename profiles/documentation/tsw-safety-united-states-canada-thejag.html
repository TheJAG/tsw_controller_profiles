Safety systems · US & Canada

Each railroad's rule book names the aspects. NORAC (Northeast Corridor, NJ Transit, Horseshoe Curve) and CROR (Canada) are speed signalling: the aspect names the speed, Limited 45, Medium 30, Slow 15 mph. GCOR (BNSF, UP, Metrolink, Caltrain, Tri-Rail) is route signalling: "Diverging" means the turnout at its posted speed. LIRR and Metro-North have their own books with the same aspects. Restricted speed: max 20 mph (Canada 15), able to stop within half the range of vision. On the corridor the cab signal rules and ACSES enforces the limits; the alerter checks that you are awake.

Safety systems

Cab signals · ATC
Pulse-code cab signals with Automatic Train Control

The cab display repeats the signal aspect and its speed. On a downgrade an alarm sounds: acknowledge within about 6 s and make a suppression brake application, or ATC applies a penalty brake.

WhereNortheast Corridor, LIRR, Metro-North, NJ Transit, MBTA, Tri-Rail; Sherman Hill (UP, buggy display)
QuadrantGear lever on the Amtrak, LIRR, Metro-North stock and the NJT Multi-Level; ENG 2 on the MBTA cab cars and the SD70ACe. NJT locos, Arrow III, Tri-Rail locos: in the cab
ACSES II
Advanced Civil Speed Enforcement System

Transponders give the civil speed limits and positive stops at home signals. The display shows the target speed; brake before the restriction. Warning: about 8 s to brake.

WhereAmtrak NEC, NJ Transit, Metro-North, LIRR, MBTA
QuadrantLeft aux square = ACSES · ATC fuse or cut-out
PTC (I-ETMS)
Positive Train Control

In the real world GPS and wayside messages enforce signals, speed limits and authorities. In TSW not enforced: the PTC fuse only lights the display.

WhereReal: BNSF, UP, Metrolink, Caltrain (I-ETMS), Tri-Rail. TSW: fuse on the F125, F59PHR, MP36, Arrow III, Tri-Rail locos
QuadrantPTC fuse on the left aux square where fitted
ATS
Automatic Train Stop (intermittent)

An inductor at each signal showing anything but Clear sounds an alarm: acknowledge (forestall) within about 8 s or the brakes apply.

WhereEx-Santa Fe lines: Metrolink San Bernardino and Antelope Valley; TSW's Tri-Rail Rotem also has it
QuadrantENG 2 ATS ack on the Rotem cab cars; F125, F59PHR, MP36: in the cab. Left aux = ATS fuse
Alerter
Driver vigilance

With no control moved for a while (shorter at speed), a light then a buzzer: reset or the train gets a penalty brake. Any control movement also resets.

WhereEvery modern locomotive and cab car
QuadrantGear lever = alerter reset on most locos; Caltrain Bi-Level: ENG 2; F7s: safety pedal / deadman. Aux square = alerter fuse

Canadian Rail Operating Rules

Canada names aspects by this and the next signal: Clear to Stop, Clear to Limited, Limited to Clear. Same lamps, different grammar (flashing green = Clear to Limited).

How to drive under cab signals, ACSES, PTC, ATS and the alerter

Cab signal downgrade (ATC)

  1. The cab display drops (e.g. Clear to Approach) and the alarm sounds.
  2. Within about 6 s: Acknowledge (see the card for your train) and put the automatic brake in Suppression (EMUs: controller to Coast and brake).
  3. Release once below the new cab-signal speed.
  4. Some trains (LIRR) want every cab-speed change acknowledged, upgrades too.

ACSES speed and stop enforcement

  1. Watch the ACSES target speed and distance on the display.
  2. When the warning sounds, acknowledge and brake to the target before the restriction.
  3. Positive stop: ACSES stops you within 1000 ft of an absolute signal at Stop. With permission press Stop Release (in the cab), then max 15 mph through the interlocking.

PTC in TSW

  • Keep the PTC fuse in where the train has one; the display may light, but TSW does not enforce PTC.
  • Drive to the wayside signals and the speed signs yourself.
  • Real I-ETMS counts down to an enforcement; brake and the warning clears.

ATS (Metrolink, Tri-Rail Rotem)

  1. Passing an inductor at any signal but Clear the ATS alarm sounds.
  2. Press ATS acknowledge within about 8 s: ENG 2 on the Rotem cab cars, in the cab on the F125, F59PHR and MP36.
  3. Then drive to the signal aspect.

Alerter

  1. Keep the alerter cut in (aux square, fuse in).
  2. When the alerter light flashes or the buzzer sounds, reset it with the gear lever (or move a control).
  3. Missed: penalty brake. Stop, brake handle to Suppression, reset, then release.

Penalty brake recovery

  1. Throttle to Idle, automatic brake handle to Suppression (or Full Service).
  2. Wait until the PCS lamp goes out (a minute or so).
  3. Release the brake, recharge, then continue.
Before departure: Alerter, ATC / ACSES (or PTC, ATS) cut in: fuses in, cut-out squares as the train's manual says. Cab signal and wayside signal agree. Check in the train's manual which control acknowledges the cab signal: gear lever, ENG 2 or the cab.
