BR 294

Cab setup

  • Battery, engine start and the gear selector (Long distance / Towing / Shunting) in the cab
  • Take the left desk over: PARK BRK up (PanelTakeOver), then sit at that desk
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares), PZB mode in the cab
  • Lights to Front 3 or Front + rear for shunting
  • Brake selection (loco / train brake) in the cab; no AFB, the flap lever is free

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal and PZB, release the handbrake in the cab
  • Führerbremsventil to Release (far end), wait for the brakes to release
  • Fahr-/Bremsschalter: push to Increase to raise power, back to Hold power to keep it
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: pull the handle past the centre to Apply (pulse) and Hold brake, or the Führerbremsventil to Apply
  • Stop: Führerbremsventil to Apply, handle to Release / decrease
