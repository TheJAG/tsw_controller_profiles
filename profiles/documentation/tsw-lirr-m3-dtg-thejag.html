M3

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK up); the reverser loads at Key Out
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • Cab signalling cut in (left aux unlit); hold the deadman pedal in the cab if it calls
  • Lights to Bright
  • Controller at Max brake (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; buzzer (ENG 1)
  • Controller through Min brake to Coast, then P1 … P4
  • Acknowledge ATC / ACSES with the gear lever
  • Brake: controller back through Coast and Min brake into the brake zone; Max brake for the stop, Emergency nearest you
