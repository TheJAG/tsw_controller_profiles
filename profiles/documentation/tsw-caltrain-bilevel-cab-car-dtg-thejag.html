Bi-Level cab car

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: cab set-up key, brake cut-in, fuel pump, generator field, engine run, alerter and PCS breakers on (the car loads with all of them off)
  • Lights to Dim + aux; parking brake off (round aux)
  • Handle at Idle, automatic brake at Full service (parked)
  • Gear lever (reverser handle press), reverser to Forward, gear lever again: the power handle is now free

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Handle away from you through Idle into P1..P8 (one notch at a time through the gates)
  • Reset the alerter with ENG 2
  • Brake: handle back through Idle and Setup into B1..B8, automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end. After any reverser move press the gear lever before powering
