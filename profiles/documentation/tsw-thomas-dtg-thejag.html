Thomas

Cab setup

  • Thomas drives on simple controls: one spring-loaded handle for power and brake, no fireman duties; the backhead is decoration
  • Reverser to Forward (speedbrake lever) at a stand
  • Headlights on (round aux)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Move the left lever into the power zone; the handle only acts while the lever moves or ENG 1 (power hold) is up
  • The train coasts when nothing is sent; brake with the lever past 0 or with ENG 2 (brake hold) up
  • Whistle on the right throttle button
