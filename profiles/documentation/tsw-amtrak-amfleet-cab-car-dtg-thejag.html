Amfleet cab car

Cab setup

  • Throttle at Off, then PARK BRK up: reverser key in (Neutral), brake mode Lead
  • Pantograph up (ENG 2), MCB closed (round aux lit)
  • ACSES / ATC and alerter in (aux squares lit); lights to Bright
  • Reverser to Forward (the throttle is dead in Neutral); automatic brake at Full service (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Throttle notch by notch (the ACS-64 at the back follows)
  • Acknowledge the alerter and cab signals with the gear lever
  • Brake: throttle to Off, automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end. No independent or dynamic brake on the cab car
