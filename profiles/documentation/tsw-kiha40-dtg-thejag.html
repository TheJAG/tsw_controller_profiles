KiHa 40

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: the four handles are plugged in and the master key turned (the train loads with every handle removed)
  • Engine: ENG 1 up for Start, back down once running
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward, ATS power on (left aux)
  • Lights to Bright
  • Brake valve at Apply, gear at Neutral, throttle at Idle (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Brake valve to Release, then Handle Off once charged
  • Gear to Hydraulic, throttle P1 … P5
  • Acknowledge ATS with the gear lever, the EB with the left throttle button
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, valve to Apply, Lap to hold; Emergency at the far end
