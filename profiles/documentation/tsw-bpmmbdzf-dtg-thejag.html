Cab setup

  • Reverser key and brake key in (PARK BRK), battery on in the cab
  • Richtungsschalter from Off to Neutral
  • Raise the pantograph and close the Hauptschalter of the pushing BR 101 from the cab
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares), PZB mode set
  • Lights to Signal lights or Headlights
  • Open the doors (RUD TRIM); the cab car has no AFB, so the flap lever is free

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, check the signal and PZB
  • Richtungsschalter to Forward
  • Führerbremsventil to Running, wait for the brakes to release
  • Fahrschalter towards Z: the loco pushes
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Führerbremsventil towards SB in steps
