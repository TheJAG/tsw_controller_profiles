BR 111

Cab setup

  • Reverser key and brake key in (PARK BRK), battery on
  • Richtungsschalter from 0 to M
  • Raise the pantograph and close the Hauptschalter in the cab
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares), PZB mode set
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, check the signal and PZB
  • Richtungsschalter to V
  • Führerbremsventil to Running, release the Zusatzbremse
  • Fahrschalter to Auf to notch up, Halt to hold, Ab to notch down
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Führerbremsventil towards Full service in steps
