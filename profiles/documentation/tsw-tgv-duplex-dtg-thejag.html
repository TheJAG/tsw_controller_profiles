TGV Duplex

Cab setup

  • Battery, pantograph (Normal) and circuit breaker in the cab
  • PARK BRK up: master switch Active, driving mode Manual
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • KVB / TVM and VACMA to Normal (aux squares lit); the train loads with them isolated
  • Lights to Normal
  • Frein direct at Apply, handle at Max brake (parked); the robinet de frein automatique stays in the cab

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Frein direct to Release, handle to 0
  • Handle past Min power into the power zone
  • Selected speed: round aux lit, set the speed on the flap lever
  • Acknowledge VACMA / KVB with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through 0 into the brake zone; frein direct to Apply at a stand
