BR 612

Cab setup

  • Reverser key and brake key in (PARK BRK); battery, engine groups 1 and 2 and the transmission on in the cab
  • Richtungsschalter from Off to Forwards
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares), GNT fuse and PZB mode in the cab
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Fahr-/Bremshebel in Off (centre band); no AFB, the flap lever is free
  • Open the doors (RUD TRIM, hold the side you need)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (release RUD TRIM), check the signal and PZB
  • Führerbremsventil to Running, wait for the brakes to release
  • Push the handle to Throttle + to raise power, back one band to Throttle hold to keep it
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Slow down: handle back past Off to Brake + (pulse) and Brake hold, or the Führerbremsventil in steps
  • Stop: Führerbremsventil to Full service, handle to Off
