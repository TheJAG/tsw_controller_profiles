MP36PH-3C

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in, brake valve to Passenger (the loco loads with the valve cut out)
  • Handle at Idle, then reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • ATS and alerter on (aux squares lit)
  • Lights to Bright
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release; independent to Released, ENG 2 up to bail off
  • Handle away from you: N1 … N8 (the game stops at N1 first)
  • Reset the alerter and the ATS with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle and Setup into the dynamic zone; automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end
