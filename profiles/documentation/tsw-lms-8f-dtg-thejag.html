LMS 8F

Cab setup

  • The AI fireman keeps the fire, dampers, blower and firebox door; no AWS on this loco
  • Cut-off to full forward gear (far end)
  • Live injector (ENG 2) when the water drops; blower (ENG 1) is mostly the fireman’s
  • Cylinder cocks open (round aux lit) for the first turns of the wheels
  • Vacuum brake at Full application (parked); the steam brake follows it. Regulator Closed

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Large ejector open (PARK BRK up) until the vacuum is up, then closed; vacuum brake to Release
  • Regulator open a little, then more; wind the cut-off back towards Mid-gear as speed rises
  • Cylinder cocks closed after the first 100 m
  • Brake with the vacuum valve, Full application at the far end
