Safety systems · Czechia

Czech main signals combine this signal's speed (the lower yellow lamp, with light strips below the head: none = 40, yellow strip = 60, one green = 80, two green = 100 km/h) and what the next signal shows (the upper lamps): green = volno, yellow = výstraha (stop), slow-flashing yellow = expect 40, fast-flashing yellow = expect 60, slow-flashing green = expect 80, fast-flashing green = expect 100. The LS cab system repeats the signals where the track carries codes; Liberec – Stará Paka has no codes, so there it only checks your vigilance. Speeds in km/h. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's ČD 750 and 843 profiles.

Safety systems

LS (LS 90 · LS IV / LVZ)
Liniový vlakový zabezpečovač

On coded track the cab repeater shows the next signal: green, yellow, yellow ring (reduced speed), red; blue = no vigilance press needed. A more restrictive code asks for a press. On uncoded track (this route) it runs the vigilance check only.

Where843: LS 90. 750: LS IV type LVZ (same behaviour)
Quadrant843: left aux = LS 90 (On / Isolated); 750: left aux = LVZ (Active / Off)
Vigilance
Kontrola bdělosti

When the blue lamp goes out, press the vigilance button within about 7 s; then a siren for 5 s more, then the emergency brake. The checks pause while the loco brake is applied.

How to drive under LS and the vigilance check

On this route (no codes)

  1. Drive to the lineside signals; the repeater shows no code.
  2. When the blue lamp goes out, press vigilance (gear lever) within about 7 s.
  3. Missed, the siren sounds: you have 5 s more, then the emergency brake.

After an LS emergency brake

  1. Stop.
  2. 843: switch LS 90 off and on (left aux twice).
  3. 750: switch the LVZ off and on at the console in the engine room (or the left aux), then release the brake.

With LS codes (main lines)

  • The repeater shows the next signal: green, yellow, yellow ring (reduced speed), red.
  • Blue lit: no press needed.
  • A more restrictive code: press vigilance at once, then brake to the signal.

Before departure

  1. Loco brake fully applied.
  2. Train protection to Start, wait for the start lamp, then On / Active (843 LS 90, 750 LVZ). The profile's left aux sets On directly; if the system does not arm, use Start in the cab first.
  3. Check the signal, close the doors, release the brake.
Remember: The lower yellow lamp and the strips below it are the speed at this signal; the upper lamp tells you what the next signal shows.
