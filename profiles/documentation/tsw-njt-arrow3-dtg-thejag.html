Arrow III

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: control plug in, brake valve and horn cock cut in (all three load out)
  • PTC / cab signal and alerter on (aux squares lit; they load cut out)
  • Deadman: ENG 2 up
  • Lights to Bright
  • Automatic brake at Full service (parked), controller at Safety

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release
  • Controller away from you from Safety: Off, Switch, P-1 … P-3 forwards (the near half is the same in reverse)
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: controller back to Off, automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end
