Safety systems · Switzerland

Swiss main signals (system L) show a Fahrbegriff, a speed for the route: FB 1 = line speed, FB 2 = 40, FB 3 = 60, FB 5 = 90, FB 6 = 40 "Kurze Fahrt" (short distance to a Halt). On the RhB the same aspects mean FB 2 = 30, FB 3 = 45, FB 6 = 30, and there is no FB 5. Distant signals announce the next main signal; Luzern – Sursee also has system N signals with numbers. Speeds in km/h. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's profiles: RhB Allegra (Berninalinie), RhB Ge 4/4 II (Arosalinie), SBB RABe 523 (Luzern – Sursee).

Safety systems

Integra-Signum
SBB intermittent train protection (TSW: ETCS STM level)

A distant at Warnung (and a main signal at Kurze Fahrt) gives a buzzer and a yellow lamp: acknowledge at once (real: within 100 m or 5 s) or the train brakes. Passing a Halt brakes at once.

ETCS Level 1
TSW: Staff Responsible, then Full Supervision (the real line runs Limited Supervision)

Select the level on the right display at a stand and confirm. Level 1 starts in SR (40 km/h to the first balise), then FS: speed monitoring and a braking curve with a warning tone. Level 0 only checks above 100 km/h.

QuadrantENG 1 = confirm the level; left aux = ETCS on
ZSI-90 / ZS
RhB Zugsicherung (TSW); today ZSI 127

Track magnets at a distant at Warnung and at Kurze Fahrt: two beeps and a flashing yellow lamp, press ZS or the emergency brake applies. Green lamp after a proceed aspect. Real RhB now runs balise-based ZSI 127.

SiSte / deadman
Sicherheitssteuerung, Totmannpedal

Allegra: SiSte, a timed vigilance like the SiFa: reset when the button lights. Ge 4/4 II: a deadman pedal, no timed alarm. RABe 523: none simulated.

How to drive

RABe 523: Signum and ETCS

  1. At a stand select the ETCS level on the right display, then confirm with ENG 1.
  2. Level 1: 40 km/h in SR to the first balise, then follow the FS speed and curve.
  3. STM / Signum: at a Warnung press Signum reset (gear lever) at once.

RhB: ZS / ZSI

  1. Switch the ZSI on (Allegra left aux; always on in the Ge 4/4 II).
  2. At a distant at Warnung or a Kurze Fahrt: two beeps, yellow lamp flashing: press ZS (Allegra ENG 1, Ge 4/4 II gear lever).
  3. Missed: red lamp and emergency brake; stop, release.

Vigilance

  1. Allegra: SiSte on (right aux); reset with the gear lever when its button lights.
  2. Ge 4/4 II: keep the deadman cut in: left aux dark.
  3. Missed: emergency brake; stop, reset, release.
Before departure: Allegra: ZSI and SiSte on (both load off; aux squares lit). Ge 4/4 II: deadman cut in (left aux dark). RABe 523: ETCS on, level selected and confirmed. Check the signal, close the doors.
