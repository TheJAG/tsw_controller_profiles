BR 204

Cab setup

  • Battery, engine start and the gear selector (Shunting / Fast) in the cab
  • PARK BRK up: brake key and master key in (the Zb3 valve is locked without it)
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares; both load off), PZB mode in the cab
  • Reverser to Forward: the Fahrschalter only moves in Forward
  • Lights to Headlight, or Shunting (white both ends)
  • Zusatzbremse at Full, Führerbremsventil at Full service

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal and PZB, release the handbrake in the cab
  • Führerbremsventil to Running (far end), then Off once released
  • Zusatzbremse to Release
  • Fahrschalter: push one Fahrstufe at a time, LL to 6
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Fahrschalter to LL, Führerbremsventil into the service zone; Emergency nearest you
