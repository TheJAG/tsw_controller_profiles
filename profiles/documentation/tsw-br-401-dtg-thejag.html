BR 401 ICE 1

Cab setup

  • Reverser key and brake key in (PARK BRK), battery on
  • Richtungsschalter from Off to Neutral
  • Raise the pantograph and close the Hauptschalter in the cab
  • SiFa, PZB and LZB on (Störschalter unlit), PZB mode set
  • Lights to Signal normal
  • AFB on (ABT button in the cab) if you drive with the speed setter; open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, check the signal, PZB and LZB
  • Richtungsschalter to Forward
  • Führerbremsventil to Running, wait for the brakes to release
  • Fahrschalter towards Max, or set the AFB speed on the flap lever
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Führerbremsventil in steps towards Full service
