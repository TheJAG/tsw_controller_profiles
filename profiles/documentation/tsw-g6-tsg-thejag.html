Vossloh G6

Cab setup

  • Battery key in (PARK BRK); engine start, gear and HLL cut-off valve in the cab
  • SiFa and PZB on (aux squares; both load off), PZB mode in the cab
  • Direction: mode switch right once = Forward (again = off), left = Reverse; the speedbrake lever is free
  • Lights to Front, Shunting or Automatic
  • Zusatzbremse at Hold, Führerbremsventil at Apply

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal and PZB, release the parking brake in the cab
  • Führerbremsventil to Release (far end), Zusatzbremse to Release
  • Joystick: push to Throttle hold, pulse Throttle + (spring) at the far end to raise power
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: joystick back through Off to Brake hold, pulse Brake + (spring) nearest you; or the valve to Apply
  • Emergency and quick release: only in the cab (spring ends of the valve)
