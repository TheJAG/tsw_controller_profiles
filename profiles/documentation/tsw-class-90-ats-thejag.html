Class 90

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK)
  • Pantograph up, then press again to close the circuit breaker (right throttle button)
  • Check AWS and DSD isolation lights are out
  • Lights to Day
  • Release the parking brake (RUD TRIM right)
  • Train brake to Full service, DRA reset

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Train brake to Running
  • Power handle to Min, then open up gradually with the heavy train
  • Rheostatic brake on the flap lever for braking on the move
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever
