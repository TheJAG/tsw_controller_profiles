Safety systems · France

On the LGV Méditerranée (Avignon TGV included) there are no lineside signals: TVM 430 shows the speed for each block in the cab, and the track carries only block marker boards. On the classic line into Marseille Saint-Charles the lineside signaux apply, with KVB checking speeds and stops and the crocodile repeating warning signals in the cab. The VACMA checks that you are awake. Speeds are in km/h. Quadrant controls are from TheJAG's TGV Duplex profile.

Safety systems

TVM 430
Transmission Voie-Machine

Cab signalling through the rails. White on green = line speed; black on a white diamond = reach this speed by the next marker; white on black = applies now; flashing = a further reduction follows. Overspeed brakes the train.

WhereLGV Méditerranée
QuadrantLeft aux square = KVB, crocodile and TVM to Normal
KVB
Contrôle de Vitesse par Balises

Beacons check the speed to stop signals and limits against a braking curve: an alert 5 km/h over, then the brake. Display codes: 00 / 000 stop ahead, P / L limits, FU it has braked.

WhereIn and around Marseille
QuadrantLeft aux square = KVB, crocodile and TVM to Normal
Crocodile
Répétition des signaux (RS)

A contact ramp at warning signals. Closed-signal warning: the alert lamp flashes (with a bip); press Acknowledge; it stays lit until you pass the signal. Open signal: a tone only. Real world: 5 s, then the brake.

WhereClassic line into Marseille
QuadrantGear lever = Acknowledge; left aux square = KVB, crocodile and TVM to Normal
VACMA
Veille Automatique à Contrôle de Maintien d'Appui

Real: hold the pedal; release and press again when the buzzer asks. In TSW: above 8 km/h a buzzer every 60 s; press Acknowledge within 5 s or the emergency brake applies.

How to drive under TVM, KVB, the crocodile and the VACMA

TVM 430 on the LGV

  1. Drive to the speed on the cab display.
  2. White diamond: brake now so you reach it by the next block marker.
  3. White on black: hold at or below it in this block.
  4. Flashing: the block after the next is slower still.
  5. 000: be able to stop at the next marker (Nf: stop there).

Crocodile and KVB near Marseille

  1. At a closed-signal warning the alert lamp flashes: press Acknowledge (gear lever).
  2. Brake for the next signal; KVB checks your curve (00 / 000 on its display).
  3. KVB overspeed: alert, then the emergency brake (FU). Stop, Acknowledge, reset the controls, drive on on sight.

VACMA

  1. Keep the VACMA at Normal (right aux square).
  2. Above 8 km/h the buzzer sounds every 60 s: press Acknowledge (gear lever) within 5 s.
  3. Missed: emergency brake. Wait for a complete stop, Acknowledge, release and drive on.

Before departure

  1. KVB / crocodile / TVM and VACMA to Normal (aux squares lit): the train loads with them isolated.
  2. Check the signal or the TVM display, and the doors.
  3. Frein direct to Release, handle into power.

At a signal at stop

  • Sémaphore (œilleton lit): stop; then on sight at max 30 km/h where allowed.
  • Carré: stop; pass only on an order from the agent-circulation.
  • Feu rouge clignotant: no stop, on sight at max 15 km/h.
