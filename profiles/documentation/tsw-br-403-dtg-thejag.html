BR 403 ICE 3

Cab setup

  • Battery on (PARK BRK)
  • Richtungsschalter to Off, raise the pantograph and close the Hauptschalter in the cab
  • SiFa, PZB and LZB isolation switches off (aux squares unlit)
  • Headlights to Normal
  • AFB V-Soll on the flap lever to the wanted speed
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, check the signal, PZB and LZB
  • Richtungsschalter to Forward
  • Bremsen to Release
  • Fahrschalter towards Max; the AFB holds the set speed
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Bremsen in steps towards Full application
