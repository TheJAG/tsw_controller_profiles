C40-8W

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: brake valve to Freight, MU-2A valve to Lead, generator field on (the loco loads cut out with the field off; no key)
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward; warning devices and dynamic brake switch on (aux squares lit)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights on (round aux)
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); handle at Idle

Cleared to depart

  • Check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release, independent to Release (ENG 2 up to bail off)
  • Handle away from you: N1 … N8
  • Reset the alerter with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Idle and Set-up into the dynamic zone; automatic brake into the service zone for the stop, Emergency at the far end
