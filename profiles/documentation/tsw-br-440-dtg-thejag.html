DB BR 440

Cab setup

  • Insert master key, master switch on (PARK BRK)
  • Handle fully forward at Max brake, train brake at Apply
  • Raise pantograph, close the main circuit breaker
  • PZB and SiFa on (aux squares)
  • Set headlights (AUTO BRK)
  • Open doors

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors
  • Check signal and PZB
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Train brake to Drive
  • Handle back through min brake to 0, then min power and continuous power
  • Brake: forward past 0 into min brake, continuous to Max brake
