CTC-5 bi-level cab car

Cab setup

  • Throttle at Idle, then PARK BRK up: reverser key in (Neutral), brake cut in
  • ACSES / ATC and alerter fuses on (aux squares lit); lights to Bright + aux
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Full service (parked); reverser to Forward

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release (it ramps through its gates), independent to Release
  • Throttle notch by notch (the loco at the back follows)
  • Acknowledge the alerter with the gear lever, cab signals with ENG 2
  • Brake: throttle to Idle, automatic brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end. No dynamic brake on the cab car
