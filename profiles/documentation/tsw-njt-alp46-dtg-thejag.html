ALP-46

Cab setup

  • PARK BRK up: reverser handle in
  • Handle at Off, then reverser from Neutral to Forward (it only moves at Off)
  • ATC / ACSES and alerter on (aux squares lit; all load cut out)
  • Lights to Bright, ditch lights auto; the pantograph loads up
  • Independent at Full, automatic brake at Service (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal; bell on (ENG 1)
  • Automatic brake to Release, independent to Release
  • Handle away from you into the power zone
  • Acknowledge the alerter and the cab signal with the gear lever
  • Brake: handle back through Off into the dynamic zone; automatic brake to Service, Lap holds; Emergency at the far end
