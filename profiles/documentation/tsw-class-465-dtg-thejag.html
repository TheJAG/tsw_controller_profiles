Class 465

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK)
  • Reverser to Neutral
  • Check AWS/TPWS and vigilance isolation lights are out
  • Headlights to Day
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)
  • Handle at B3

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, wait for the guard buzzer
  • Check the signal
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Handle from B3 through Off to P1
  • Notch up to P4 as speed rises
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever
