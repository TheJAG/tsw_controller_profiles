BR 642

Cab setup

  • Master key on (PARK BRK); battery on in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • SiFa on and PZB fuse on (aux squares), PZB mode in the cab
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Fahr-/Bremshebel in the brake zone (nearest you), Führerbremsventil at Hold; no AFB, the flap lever is free
  • Open the doors (RUD TRIM, hold the side you need)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (release RUD TRIM), check the signal and PZB
  • Führerbremsventil to Driving (far end)
  • Fahr-/Bremshebel through 0 into the traction zone, further for more power
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: Fahr-/Bremshebel back through 0 into the brake zone, pull further for more
  • Stop: Fahr-/Bremshebel to full brake (nearest you)
