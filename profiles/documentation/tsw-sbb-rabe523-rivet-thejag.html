RABe 523 FLIRT

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK up); the handle ignores the lever without it; pantograph in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • ETCS on (left aux), level in the cab
  • Lights to Day
  • Zugbremse at Full service, handle at Max brake (parked)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Zugbremse to Release, handle to 0
  • Handle past Min power into the power zone; the Tempomat rocker stays in the cab
  • Acknowledge Integra-Signum with the gear lever, the ETCS with ENG 1
  • Brake: handle back through 0 into the brake zone; Zugbremse into the service zone; Emergency at the far ends
