Class 395

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK)
  • Pantograph up / shoes down (right throttle button), AC or DC as the line needs
  • Reverser to Neutral
  • Check AWS/TPWS and DSD isolation lights are out
  • Headlights to Day
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, wait for the guard buzzer
  • Check the signal
  • Reverser to Forward
  • Handle from Max brake through Off to P1
  • Push on to P4 as speed rises
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever
