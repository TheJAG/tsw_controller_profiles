Ge 4/4 II

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK up); pantograph, main switch and compressor in the cab
  • Reverser from Neutral to Forward
  • Deadman cut-out light out (left aux)
  • Lights to Headlights 3
  • Loco brake at Full, vacuum brake at Full service (parked); handwheel at Off

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal
  • Vacuum brake to Release, then Minimum; loco brake to Release
  • Handwheel from Off into the power zone, notch by notch
  • Acknowledge the ZSI with the gear lever
  • Brake: handwheel back through Off into the electric brake zone, vacuum brake into the service zone; Emergency at the far end
