4024 Talent

Cab setup

  • Battery and pantograph in the cab
  • PARK BRK up: cab active and brake key in
  • Reverser from Off to Forward
  • SiFa and PZB fuses on (aux squares), PZB mode in the cab
  • Lights to Headlights
  • Brake valve at Apply, handle at Max brake (parked); the Tempomat stays in the cab

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors (RUD TRIM), check the signal and PZB
  • Brake valve to Drive, handle to 0, wait for the release
  • Handle past Min power into the power zone
  • Acknowledge SiFa with the gear lever, PZB with the ENG switches
  • Brake: handle back through 0 into the brake zone, or the brake valve to Apply
  • Stop: brake valve to Apply, handle to Max brake
