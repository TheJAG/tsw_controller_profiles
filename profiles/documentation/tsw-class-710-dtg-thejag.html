Class 710

Cab setup

  • Master key in (PARK BRK)
  • Master control switch from Shutdown to Secure, then Forward when ready
  • Pantograph up (right throttle button) under the wires
  • Check TPWS/AWS and DSD isolation lights are out
  • Headlights to Day
  • Open doors (RUD TRIM)

Cleared to depart

  • Close doors, wait for the signal buzzer
  • Check the signal
  • Master control switch to Forward
  • Handle from Max brake up to Coast
  • Push into the traction zone, more as speed rises
  • Acknowledge AWS with the gear lever
