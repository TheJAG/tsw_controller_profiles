# Spawn mode for a capture (secondary path)

Read this only when the brief says "spawn". The timetable loop in `CAPTURE_AGENT.md` is the default (user decision 2026-10-11). Spawn mode suits a quick re-capture of one lever on a known train, or a train with no daytime service.

It was tried on the BR 147 (2026-10-11) and works, but the user found it awkward and it has two drawbacks the service loop has not: a spawned loco starts with cold keys (brake valve cut out, desk inactive, safety systems isolated), which `capture_train.js` does not recognise and which locked the 147's power handle until it was probed by hand; and placement is blind (the ghost lands on the nearest track, you walk to the door, and a multi-car unit must be spawned with the wanted cab car leading). One free-roam session on a route with a large electrified yard in daylight serves every train: the spawn list shows all owned stock on any route (UK units and tube stock appear beside the DB locos on Leipzig – Dresden). Home screen to sitting in the cab takes under 2 minutes; the BR 147 took 18 min in all against the BR 146's 20 by the service route, most of it the sweep and the probe.

1. Home: To the Trains (1340,620), wait 3 s; Choose a Route (970,780), wait 4 s; the route tile (Leipzig – Dresden is first while it is the most recently played), wait 4 s; **Free Roam** (2566,720), wait 5 s.
2. Free Roam screen: Start At ▶ at (2136,338) cycles the locations (Leipzig Hbf → Sellerhausen → Paunsdorf → **Engelsdorf**: 3 clicks). Time of Day ▶▶ at (2124,422) adds 5 min per click (24 clicks take 10:00 to 12:00). Weather reads Custom / Clear by default. PLAY NOW (2516,1158), wait 60 s: you stand on foot on the platform.
3. **Tab** (`key 9`) opens a radial menu: Spawn New Train (1530,722), Respawn Last (top), Delete Train (right). Click Spawn New Train, wait 4 s, click the search box (1256,338), `keys 147`, click the result (790,494). Scroll the formation list with the wheel over (2480,900), click the wanted formation ("[Light Loco]" at (2400,1026) was the last one for the 147; for a unit pick the formation whose first vehicle is the cab car you want), then SPAWN (2490,1170).
4. Walk (`hold 87 5000` = W) until the green ghost train shows on the track, then **X** (`key 88`) places it. The loco comes up powered: pantograph raised, screens lit, catenary present at Engelsdorf.
5. Walk to the cab door, click the handle twice (unlock, open), look up at the doorstep and press E ("Climb Up"). Inside, walk past the seat to the desk, turn round (`-Command "& ./tools/tsw_input.ps1 mmove '-500' '150'"`) and rest the crosshair on the seat cushion from the front; after about a second the prompt **"E Sit in Driver's Seat"** appears, press E (`key 69`). The cab camera and the API come up at once. There is no take-control entry anywhere else (radial, pause menu, map "Sandbox Options", Fast Travel): the seat prompt is the only way, and it only shows from the front of the seat.
6. Continue with step 7 of the loop in `CAPTURE_AGENT.md` (API check, desk photo, capture). A spawned loco is cold in the sense of its keys: the BR 147 had its brake valve cut out (`DriversBrakeCutoff_F` 1 = Cut-Out), the desk inactive (`ActivateDriversCompartment_F` push button, MasterSwitch) and the PZB / SiFa / LZB back-wall switches Isolated, and its combined `MasterController_F` stored nothing above 0.1 until the desk was activated and the valve cut in. If a main lever stays at one notch, set those key-like nodes and probe as `CAPTURE_AGENT.md` describes.
7. Pitfalls of this mode: `X` does nothing from far away; the loco lands on the track nearest the ghost (the 147 came out on Engelsdorf Pl 1, a main-line platform, fine for a capture); negative `mmove` values need the `-Command` form; the pause menu's Restart tile reads "On Foot".
